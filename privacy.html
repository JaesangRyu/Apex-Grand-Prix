Payments. Card details are entered on Stripe's secure page and never reach the game. Stripe's privacy policy applies to payment data.

Multiplayer. Online races connect players directly (WebRTC). Like any direct connection, your IP address is visible to the other players in your room and to the connection services we use (PeerJS signaling and a TURN relay). We do not store IP addresses.

Usage analytics (Microsoft Clarity). To find confusing screens and fix them, we work with Microsoft Clarity to see how players use the game's menus and screens: taps, clicks, scrolling, which screen is open, whether a race was started or finished, device type, browser, language and approximate country. This is collected through first-party and third-party cookies and similar technologies and is used for site optimization and product improvement. Text you type (such as your nickname) is masked and not recorded. For more about what Microsoft collects and how it uses data, see the Microsoft Privacy Statement. You can turn this off at any time in Settings → ℹ Info → Usage analytics, and it is not loaded when your browser sends a Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track signal.

Other services. The game may load fonts from Google Fonts. We do not show ads and do not use advertising trackers.

2. What we do not do

We do not sell or share your personal information for advertising. Please do not use your real name or contact details as a nickname; nicknames are public.

3. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: Settings → ℹ Info → Account → Delete account. This deletes your sign-in account and your paid gem balance, items and purchase history on our server.
  • Erase device data: Settings → ℹ Info → Erase game data on this device.
  • Leaderboard entries: email us to remove a record or nickname. You can also report other players' names with 🚩.
  • California and other US residents may request access to or deletion of their information at the contact above. We will not discriminate against you for making a request.

4. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

5. Retention

Leaderboard records stay until removed by request or moderation. Account and purchase data stay until you delete your account; Stripe keeps payment records as required by law.

6. Changes

We will update this page and the effective date when this policy changes.
